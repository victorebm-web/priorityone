How the score works

Score = how much the account matters × how urgent its signals are

How much the account matters

Bigger accounts count more, so the same problem ranks higher on a big customer.

{# What the colours mean #}

What the colours on "Your book" mean

  • Needs attention. An urgent signal is live and you haven't handled it this week. Includes urgent accounts that didn't fit in your {{ sc.MAX_WEEKLY_ITEMS }}.
  • Worth a look. An opportunity, or housekeeping such as an overdue QBR or an account that has gone quiet.
  • On track. Nothing open, or you've already handled it or set it aside this week.

Within each colour, the book is ordered by what's at stake: revenue run rate × the signal's weight. Setting an account aside drops its score to {{ (sc.SUPPRESSION_FACTOR * 100) | int }}% for {{ sc.SUPPRESSION_LOOKBACK_WEEKS }} weeks; it comes back early if it gets more than {{ (sc.RESURFACE_THRESHOLD * 100) | int }}% worse.
